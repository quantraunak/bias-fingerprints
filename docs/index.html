The long decile runs a beta of 1.10 against the short decile's 0.99 — a tilt positive in 73% of months, over a window in which the market compounded at 13.7%. Once it is removed, what remains is not statistically separable from zero.

Across six model seeds with identical economics, IC holds at 0.0164 ± 0.0008 while Sharpe ranges from 0.09 to 0.49. The signal is stable; the Sharpe is a draw from a distribution, and every performance figure above should be read against that spread.
